## Limitations

- Market coordinates are town centroids from GeoNames, matched by name (see `dataset/crop_price/market_locations.csv`); they are not surveyed yard positions.
- Reanalysis grid cells are ~25 km; neighbouring markets can share a cell.
- The state-level series uses the plain mean of all mapped markets' weather.
- No archived day-ahead weather forecasts are used, so no weather *forecast* feature exists in the model.
